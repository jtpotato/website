This blog lives directly in the Next.js app and uses MDX files for content.

I wanted a setup that is simple to maintain:

- Each post is just a file in `content/blog`.
- Frontmatter controls title, date, excerpt, and tags.
- The website reads posts at build time and renders them as static pages.

Future posts will focus on STEM projects, competitions, and things I learn while building.
